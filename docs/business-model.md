- loan underwriting
- credit assessment

## Revenue

- origination fee
- underwriting fee

## Trust Controls

- no loan disbursement above the applicant's registered underwriting-approved amount without governor gate
- credit assessment must be completed before any loan approval
- underwriting records are auditable, not editable
